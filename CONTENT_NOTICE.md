## What this is

An unofficial, fan-made quick reference for the tabletop game *Warhammer 40000*. The sheets
condense and abbreviate the rules into short reminders. They are not a reproduction of the
official rulebooks and do not replace them: where a sheet and the official rules disagree, the
official rules win. Section numbers in parentheses, such as "(08.02)", point to the matching section
of the official rules purely as a reference.

The sheets contain no Games Workshop logos, artwork, or other graphics.

## Origin
